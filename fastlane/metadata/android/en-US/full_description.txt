<p><b>Nocturne</b> — 夜色如歌。A Material 3 music player where local audio and streaming sing in harmony.</p>

<p>Nocturne brings your treasured FLAC collection and millions of streaming tracks together in one elegant interface. From local audio files to online music services, every listening session becomes a delightful ritual.</p>

<p><br><b>Features:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Local audio file playback (MP3, OGG, FLAC, etc.) with a custom tag extractor for accurate metadata</li>
<li>Online music streaming — search, play, and download, ad-free background playback</li>
<li>Account synchronization — two-way sync of playlists, subscriptions, and favorites</li>
<li>Play local and streaming songs at the same time</li>
<li>Multiple queues — manage several playback queues simultaneously</li>
<li>Synchronized lyrics, and support for word-by-word/karaoke lyrics (LRC, TTML)</li>
<li>Audio normalization, tempo/pitch adjustment, and various audio effects</li>
<li>Android Auto support</li>
<li>Material 3 dynamic color that adapts to your device theme</li>
</ul>

<p><br><b>Notice:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Android 8 (Oreo) and higher is supported</li>
<li>Nocturne is in active development. Please report issues on our GitHub page</li>
</ul>
