# Nocturne

<img src="./assets/icon.png" height="88" alt="Nocturne app icon">

A Material 3 music player for Android — 夜色如歌

## Features

Nocturne is a music player that brings local audio and streaming music together in perfect harmony.

- **Local playback** — MP3, OGG, FLAC and more, with a custom tag extractor for accurate metadata
- **Streaming** — search, play, and download from online sources, ad-free background playback
- **Account sync** — two-way sync of playlists, subscriptions, and favorites
- **Multiple queues** — manage several playback queues simultaneously
- **Synchronized lyrics** — line-by-line and word-by-word (karaoke), LRC &amp; TTML
- **Audio enhancement** — loudness normalization, tempo/pitch, equalizer
- **Android Auto** — seamless driving integration
- **Material 3** — immersive dynamic color that adapts to your device

> [!NOTE]
> Android 8 (Oreo) and higher is supported.
